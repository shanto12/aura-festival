✦ 01 / The experience Est. 2022 — Lisbon, PT

Not a festival.
A frequency.

For three nights, a decommissioned power station becomes a living instrument. Sound moves through architecture. Light behaves like weather. Strangers become a temporary constellation.

AURA is a meeting place for music, art and the people who make both feel inevitable. No barriers, no big screens — just bodies, bass and a building learning to breathe.
